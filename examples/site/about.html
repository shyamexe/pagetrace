About the bakery

The starter was born in a Fort Kochi kitchen in 2011, fed on nothing but flour and water, and it has been fed every single day since then without exception.

The flour

We buy from a mill outside Palakkad that stone grinds wheat in small batches, which means the flour varies through the year and so does the bread, and we think that is the point rather than a problem to engineer away.

The people

Four bakers work the overnight shift, and two more run the counter through the day. Everyone who works here learns to shape a loaf, whatever they were hired to do.

Why we close early

We bake a fixed quantity each day and stop when it is gone, rather than baking through the afternoon to keep shelves full. It means turning people away some days, and it also means nothing sits under a lamp for six hours pretending to be fresh.
