Sourdough baked daily in Kochi

Northwind Bakery has been baking naturally leavened sourdough in Fort Kochi since 2011, using a starter kept alive for over a decade and flour milled in Kerala.

What we bake

Sourdough loaves come out of the oven at seven each morning, followed by croissants at eight and cardamom buns by nine. Everything is made the same day it is sold, and whatever is left at closing goes to the shelter on Princess Street.

Finding us

We are on Burgher Street, two doors down from the post office, open from seven in the morning until the shelves are empty, which is usually mid afternoon on weekends.

Ordering ahead

Whole loaves can be reserved by phone the day before, which is worth doing on Saturdays when the first bake is usually gone by half past eight. We do not take payment in advance and we hold reserved bread until noon, after which it goes back on the shelf for whoever is there.
