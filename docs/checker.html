    Where the CLI goes further

    Whole-site mode crawls a handful of pages through a public proxy, which is enough to show duplicate titles, hreflang gaps, dead links and sitemap health. The CLI crawls the whole sitemap with no proxy in the way, checks outbound links, and — the part no checker can do — diffs today's surface against the one committed last week, so CI fails on what this deploy changed. 

    npx pagetrace audit --url https://example.com
npx pagetrace links --url https://example.com --external
